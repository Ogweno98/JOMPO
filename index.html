About JOMPO

JOMPO Digital Solutions is a youth-focused organization working at the intersection of community development, sustainable livelihoods, environmental action and digital innovation.

Who We Are

JOMPO brings together people, ideas and technology to respond to practical community challenges. Our work is grounded in locally relevant action and strengthened through partnerships that create opportunities for young people and communities.

We are building an organization that can learn from implementation, document results and grow responsible partnerships around solutions that have practical value.

Our partnership principle: we aim to turn resources into practical action, evidence and learning that can support sustainable community impact.

Featured Project

Global Youth Mobilization – Agriculture & Agro-Forestry Project

This project is implemented by JOMPO Digital Solutions through the Global Youth Mobilization (GYM) Youth Empowerment Fund. It focuses on practical youth engagement in sustainable agriculture and agro-forestry.

The project brings young people into hands-on activities that connect food production, environmental stewardship, skills development and community participation.

A Practical Funding Proposition

JOMPO is open to partnership models that match the stage, ambition and requirements of each programme. Rather than applying one funding model to every opportunity, we can develop project-specific scopes, budgets, milestones and reporting arrangements with partners.

EARLY / PILOT SUPPORT

Test & Learn

Support a focused activity or digital product improvement where the priority is to test an approach, gather user feedback and document lessons.

  • Focused implementation
  • User engagement and feedback
  • Learning documentation
  • Early evidence generation
PROJECT PARTNERSHIP

Implement & Measure

Support a defined JOMPO project with agreed activities, outputs, milestones and monitoring so that implementation can be strengthened and results documented.

  • Community and youth activities
  • Project coordination
  • Monitoring and reporting
  • Partner visibility and learning
GROWTH / SCALE

Strengthen & Expand

Support organizational and programme capacity needed to build on approaches that show promise and prepare them for responsible expansion.

  • Systems and capacity
  • Digital solution refinement
  • Evidence and learning
  • Strategic partnerships

What we can prepare for a funder

For a serious funding conversation, JOMPO can work with a partner to define the problem, target participants, activities, implementation timeline, budget, measurable outputs and reporting expectations for the proposed intervention. This keeps the funding request specific rather than presenting a one-size-fits-all figure.

Important: Funding amounts should be agreed from a project-specific budget and scope; the website does not present invented figures.
